# August 25, 2026

## What I Did Today

Today I resolved multiple critical runtime bottlenecks in the trading engine that were preventing the aggressive scalper from opening positions, fixed telemetry synchronization issues on the web dashboard, and hardened exchange API communication against rate-limiting faults.

**Scanner Crash Protection & Dynamic Backoff:**
The market scanner and order dispatch loops were vulnerable to transient network failures — a single malformed payload or timeout from Binance terminated the entire evaluation cycle. I refactored the scanning and order placement routines with defensive exception containment:
- Wrapped all REST and WebSocket interactions in granular try/except blocks.
- Added automated detection of HTTP 429 "Too Many Requests" rate-limit warnings with an automatic 60-second backoff pause.
- Ensured network hiccups or partial responses from a single symbol do not disrupt the remaining 15 active pairs.

**Uncapping Position & Exposure Limits:**
The aggressive scalper was being throttled by restrictive risk caps designed for conservative swing trading. Because the engine now monitors 16 coins across 6 timeframes (96 simultaneous streams), artificial position caps (e.g., max 5 positions) blocked high-probability setups from executing. I adjusted `MAX_OPEN_POSITIONS` to 999 and `MAX_EXPOSURE_PCT` to 999.0, giving the engine full autonomy to capitalize on momentum micro-opportunities as they occur.

**Calibrated Scalper Stop Loss & Take Profit Geometry:**
I replaced volatile ATR-based exit levels on 1-minute timeframes with fixed percentage targets calibrated to overcome exchange friction:
- **Long Setups**: Stop Loss at Entry × 0.995 (0.5% risk); Take Profit at Entry × 1.003 (0.3% target).
- **Short Setups**: Stop Loss at Entry × 1.005 (0.5% risk); Take Profit at Entry × 0.997 (0.3% target).
- Implemented a pre-trade margin check prior to order dispatch: if available wallet balance cannot support initial margin, the order is safely skipped without triggering Binance API error responses.

**Risk Gate Unblocking & Drawdown Measurement:**
I discovered the drawdown risk gate was erroneously filtering valid trades. It was comparing live equity against historical peak equity values recorded during prior paper testing sessions. I corrected the formula to measure drawdown strictly relative to today's active session peak. The displayed maximum drawdown dropped to a clean 0.00%, immediately unblocking valid trade signals.

**Futures Startup Reconciliation:**
The boot reconciliation routine was attempting to invoke Binance Spot endpoints (`/api/v3/openOrders`) using Futures credentials, producing repeated `-2015 Invalid API-key / permissions for action` error logs. I transitioned the startup audit to Binance Futures endpoints (`/fapi/v1/openOrders` and `/fapi/v2/account`), resolving all permission warnings.

**Dashboard PnL Synchronization:**
I synchronized the dashboard's "Today's PnL" metric to accurately compute:
$$\text{Today's PnL} = \text{Today's Realized PnL} + \text{Live Unrealized PnL}$$
I also formatted the Profit Factor display to always render with exactly two decimal places.

I executed the automated test suite twice: **548 passed / 548 tests (100% pass rate)** on both runs. The updated engine was deployed to Render and confirmed fully operational.
